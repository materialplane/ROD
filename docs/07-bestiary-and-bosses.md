# ROD — Bestiary & The 8 Dimensional Portal Bosses

> **Status:** Complete (Shared-Engine Rules, All 8 Portal Realm Bosses, All 120 SRD 5.2 Portal Mobs & Ethoria Natives Locked)  
> **Cosmology Rule:** **Ethoria** is a strictly human world. King Garrick's dark ritual tore open **8 dimensional portals**, each linking to a **D&D Plane of Existence / Realm** (populated by multiple Creature Types forming that realm's ecology and ruled by its apex boss, ending with **King Garrick** commanding the Shadowfell's Undead legion).

---

## 1. Unified Enemy Engine Rules *(Same Stats & Combat Rules as Heroes)*

To keep combat 100% consistent and balanced across the entire game:
* **Shared 5 Attributes & 9 Damage Types:** Every enemy and boss uses the exact same `Force, Finesse, Grit, Arcana, Presence` attributes, timeline speed, and 9 damage-type rules as the 6 player classes.
* **Enemy Move Pool (`2–3` Max Abilities per Mob):** Enemies keep lean, readable move lists alongside their basic attacks (`reusing caster spell mechanics or clean monster actions without custom one-off rule bloat`):
  * **Normal Mobs & Elites:** Have basic `0 MP` attacks plus **2–3 signature monster abilities max**.
  * **Portal Bosses:** Have basic attacks plus **3–4 signature boss abilities**, paired with **Guard Points** and **Telegraphed** boss moves.

### Boss #1 — **The Ashen Iron Golem** *(SRD 5.2: `Construct`)*
* **Location:** **Map 1 · Zone 3** *(The Crownlands)* — *The Ashen Gate* *(Portal #1 · Requires `Ashen Forge Keystone` from `Q025`)* | **Target Level:** `11–15` | **Reward:** `1,200 XP` | `650s`
* **What It Is:** A towering 12-foot **animated iron statue** built inside a lifeless volcanic forge dimension—solid blackened iron plates, glowing furnace seams, and no soul or mind.
* **Guard Points:** `3` | **Weaknesses:** **Bludgeoning, Frost, Radiant** | **Resists:** Fire, Poison *(Immune to Poison ticks)*

  1. **Furnace Slam** — Heavy **Bludgeoning** strike that chips hero Physical Defense.

### Boss #8 *(Final Boss)* — **King Garrick, Sovereign of the Undead Eclipse** *(SRD 5.2: `Undead` Dimension)*
* **Location:** **Map 4 · Zone 20** *(The Dreadwaste)* — *The Eclipse Gate (Heart of Black Keep)* *(Portal #8 · Requires `Interlocking Wolf Pendant` from `Q178`)* | **Target Level:** `96–99` | **Reward:** `75,000 XP` | `120,000s`
* **What It Is:** **King Garrick**—the lone human of **Ethoria** who forced open the **8th Undead Dimension** to raise his undead army, absorbing Lich/Death-Knight dark magic onto his own massive scarred frame after mocking **Koren & Ellowyn's stolen silver wolf pendant** (taken off Valen in the coliseum and entrusted to the **Abyssal Eclipse Guard** as the final ward-key locking **The Eclipse Gate**).
* **Party Structure:** All **6 surviving heroes converge** at Black Keep; the player fights the entire battle with their chosen **4-hero squad intact**.

#### Phase 1 — *The Iron Tyrant*
* **Guard Points:** `5` | **Weaknesses:** **Radiant, Piercing, Lightning** | **Resists:** Slashing, Shadow
* **Boss Abilities:**
  1. **Executioner's Greatsword** — Relentless multi-hit **Slashing** combo on 1 hero.
  2. **Risen Vanguard** — Calls 2 **Wight/Skeleton** guards onto the field and raises Physical Defense.
  3. **Necrotic Lance** *(uses Ellowyn's **Umbral Lance** mechanic)* — Dark spear piercing 1 hero with **Shadow + Curse**.

#### Phase 2 — *Vessel of the Undead Eclipse* *(Triggered at `<55% HP`)*
* **Guard Points:** `6` | **Weaknesses:** **Radiant, Fire, Bludgeoning** | **Resists:** Shadow, Frost, Poison
* **Boss Abilities:**
  1. **Eclipse Cataclysm *(Telegraphed)*** *(uses Ellowyn's **Astral Judgment** / **Eclipse Singularity** mechanic)* — Massive party-wide **Shadow + Fire** devastation.
  2. **Timeless Chill** *(uses Ellowyn's **Blizzard Vortex** mechanic)* — Inflicts **Chill** across the party, pushing turns back.
  3. **Undying Vow** *(uses Harlan's **Undying Vow** mechanic)* — Raises a dark survival barrier and punishes reckless strikes.

#### Story Climax *(End of Battle)*
* As King Garrick's Phase 2 HP breaks, he launches one final desperate dark strike aimed straight at **Rowan**. **Commander Orin** throws himself in front of his son to intercept the fatal blow—**dying in Rowan's arms** as Valen and the party shatter Garrick and collapse the 8th and final portal.
* **Story Climax Payoff:** Unsealed by reclaiming **The Interlocking Wolf Pendant** in **Quest #178**, settling Koren, Ellowyn, and Valen's blood debt against the Tyrant King.
