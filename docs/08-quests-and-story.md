# ROD — Quests, Story Arcs & Sandbox Progression (`178` Quests Across `40` Hubs)

> **Status:** Complete (`4–5` Handcrafted Quests at Every One of the `40` Settlements & Castles | `178` Total Quests)  
> **Core Rule:** Only the **`40` Hubs + `20` Unique Zone Outposts + `8` Portals** (`17` per map) are permanent Overworld markers. All `178` unique Quest `.webp` tokens stay active as **illustrated Quest Journal cards**, split cleanly across three launch formats: **`151` Wilderness Quest Dungeons/Sites (`wilderness_pin`, temporary field pins that appear near their Hub only while active and clear when completed)**, **`16` Hub-Launched Dungeons & Keep Raids (`hub_interior`, launched directly via an action button inside a Hub/Keep's static `16:9` dialogue screen into a multi-room `1:1` instance)**, and **`11` Hub-to-Hub Couriers (`hub_courier`, carried across the Overworld and completed at the destination Hub)**.  
> **No-One-Note-Battle & Static-Hub Rule:** Because Towns, Harbors, and Keeps are static `16:9` dialogue/shop screens with NPC cards and buttons, combat **never** happens inside a Hub menu screen—and Wilderness quests **never** play out as "walk to a dot and immediately pop open a single flat battle modal." Every Wilderness Quest Site (`151`) and Hub-Launched Raid/Dungeon (`16`) opens a **Procedural `1:1` Zelda-Style Dungeon / Tactical Area**, while Courier runs (`11`) and Zone Outpost Hunts (`20` Outposts) play out organically across the Overworld.  
> **Global Active Quest Limit (`15` Active Quests Total):** Instead of a separate repeatable quest cap, players share a single **Global Active Quest Limit (`15` active quests max across Hub Quests and Zone Outpost Hunts combined)** and may abandon or turn in hunts anytime to free slots.  
> **XP & Silver Rule:** Rewards scale cleanly across Ethoria's **`20` Level Zones (`docs/03`)** so clearing local quests on each unlocked continent half (`~17–28` quests per Map half) naturally carries the party across its Level Zone band alongside Portal encounters and **Zone Outpost Hunts**.

## How Quests Play Out (`1:1` Zelda Dungeons & Raids vs. Overworld Quests)

1. **Procedural `1:1` Zelda-Style Dungeons & Raids *(`151` Wilderness Quests + `16` Hub-Launched Raids + All `8` Portals)*:**
   * **Classic Zelda Room-Grid Inspiration:** Both indoor locations *(Highcrown's aqueducts, mines, catacombs, keep dungeons/halls, sunken ships)* AND outdoor wilderness zones *(wolf woods, orchard bandit camps, reefs, pier blockades, canyon ambushes, haunted glades)* plus all **8 Dimensional Portals** generate a real explorable multi-room top-down instance.
   * **Grid Size = Bounding Canvas, Not Packed Rooms:** Bounding grids span anywhere from **`2×2`** (compact skirmish area/lair) up to **`10×10+`** (sprawling strongholds & Portals). Just like original *The Legend of Zelda*, a **`10×10` grid does NOT create 100 rooms**—it simply bounds the layout while the generator carves twisting paths, side wings, locked doors/barricades, loops, and **dead ends** through only a portion of those grid cells (`leaving empty uncarved space around branches`).
   * **`~10`-Tile `1:1` Doorless Room Pools:** Instead of custom art per room, each environment theme (*Stone Aqueduct / Sewers, Limestone Cave, Woodland Thicket / Clearing, Ancient Ruin / Shrine, Coastal Reef / Shipwreck / Harbor Pier, Outlaw Stockade / Keep*, plus the **8 Portal Planes**) maintains a pool of **`~10` doorless `1:1` square top-down paintings**. The generator places tiles across the carved path and dynamically overlays **N/S/E/W doors, keys & locked gates, dead-end walls/barricades, loot chests, patrolling encounters, and the final Mini-Boss / Objective room**.
2. **Hub-Launched Dungeons & Keep Raids (`16` Quests Inside Static `16:9` Hubs):**
   * Because Hub interiors (`LocationInteriorModal`) are static `16:9` screens with NPC lists, dialogue, inns, and shops, quests such as *Seal the Aqueduct Grate*, *Pier Three Blockade*, and assaults inside *Hollowstone Keep, The Gilded Auction Manor, Bleakwatch Citadel,* and *Garrick's Black Keep* never try to simulate fighting inside the menu itself—accepting/selecting them provides a **Launch Raid / Enter Dungeon** action button inside that Hub (`or at Highcrown's grate`) that transitions cleanly into that quest's own **`1:1` Zelda-style multi-room instance**.
3. **Overworld Hub-to-Hub Couriers (`11` Quests) & Zone Outpost Hunts (`20` Outposts):**
   * **Couriers (`11` Quests):** Carry sealed dispatches, antitoxin, or siege bolts across the Overworld from Origin Hub to Destination Hub *(with optional road-ambush clearing along the route)* and deliver in person at the destination settlement.
   * **Zone Outpost Hunts (`20` Outposts):** Pure Overworld biome hunting with **zero map pins** and **zero spawn boosting**.

*(Every temporary Overworld Quest Site spawns inside its matching Level Zone, inset **`12+` painted hexes** away from open neighboring Zone borders, and reachable on foot from its Hub without crossing higher-level Zones.)*

## How the 4-Layer Quest Structure Works

1. **Layer 1 — Open 8-Portal Main Goal, Fear-Gated Crossings & Catalysts:** All **8 Portals** sit in **`Zone 3` & `Zone 5` of each map** (`disappearing cleanly when beaten`), and opening each Portal requires its local **Portal Catalyst** Key Item awarded by the apex quest in that Portal's zone (`Q025` Ashen Forge Keystone, `Q044` Abyssal Brine-Pearl, `Q067` Blood-Iron Keystone, `Q089` Spore-Heart Amber, `Q111` Brass Cinder-Seal, `Q133` Storm-Crown Lodestone, `Q155` Pale-Abyss Ice-Seal, `Q178` Interlocking Wolf Pendant). Inside any unlocked landmass, towns and quests stay open sandbox exploration, while channel/mountain crossings reflect fear and fortress containment: **nobody dares ferry across inland waterways on Maps 1–3 until that map's 1st Portal (`#1, #3, #5`) is destroyed**, **no sea captain sails the open sea from Maps `1–3` until that map's last Portal (`#2, #4, #6`) is destroyed** (`leading onward cleanly to Maps 2–4`), and **Map 4's Border Mountain Pass Gates** into mountain-enclosed **Dreadwaste & Black Keep (`Zones 19–20`)** unlock once **Portal #7 (`The Pale Abyss`)** falls. Because **Map 4** ends the campaign at **Portal #8**, **Arctic Sea-Port Town** simply offers return voyages back to **Maps 1, 2, or 3**. Defeating **Boss #8 King Garrick** completes the campaign.
2. **Layer 2 — Independent Multi-Step Storylines (`4` Chains / `11` Core Steps):** Cast & rival arcs woven seamlessly into Hub quests across Ethoria.
3. **Layer 3 — Standalone Hub Quests (`167` Quests — `4–5` Per Hub):** Every Town, Village, Outpost, Fort, and Castle holds **`4–5` purpose-driven quests** (`[Rescue]`, `[Retrieve]`, `[Deliver]`, `[Escort / Defend]`, `[Investigate]`, `[Purge / Restore]`) ending in a named **Mini-Boss** + **Silver + XP + Pick 1 of 3 Carved Runestones**.
4. **Layer 4 — Repeatable Zone Outpost Hunts (`20` Unique Outposts — Zero Map Pins, Zero Spawn Rigging):**
   * Each of the **`20` painted 5-level Zones** (`5` per map) houses **1 permanent unique Zone Outpost** (`see docs/04`).
   * Talking to that Outpost's **Hunt Quartermaster** randomly picks **1 monster species from that specific Zone's spawn list** to hunt `X` of (`4–8` kills depending on enemy tier).
   * **Zero Quest Pins & Zero Spawn Rate Boosts:** No map marker is placed, and the target monster does **not** spawn any more frequently. Kills earned during normal exploration in that Zone count automatically toward the quest, and having an active Zone Outpost Hunt *(or Hub Quest)* in that Zone bypasses hidden **Zone Stealth** (`see docs/03`) so spawns run at full `100%` normal speed.
   * **Biome Mastery Loop:** Because each enemy species spawns in matching painted **terrain biomes** inside that Zone (e.g., *Wolves* in woods, *Hydras* in deep marshes, *Wyverns* on granite ridges, *Basilisks* on sandstone mesas), observant players learn where monsters naturally inhabit Ethoria and target those biomes to complete hunts quickly—using the live **Biome** readout in the Overworld HUD (`see docs/09`). Tiny shoreline slivers such as **Map 4's `Frost-Shore Beaches`** pull spawns from their adjacent mainland Zone biomes rather than gating unique mobs on beach tiles.
   * **Rewards:** **XP + Silver + Consumables** (`Potions` & `Ailment Cures` scaled to Zone tier — zero Runestones).
   * **Unlimited Contracts & Global Steam Milestones:** Individual Zone Outposts never cut off permanently—players can continue taking hunts anywhere as needed for XP, Silver, or Consumables—while a global counter tracks total completed **Zone Outpost Hunts** across all `20` Outposts for **Steam Achievements at `25`, `100`, `200`, and `500`** (`see docs/09`).
