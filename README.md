# ROD — Project Hub

An original turn-based, illustrated-world RPG set across the human world of **Ethoria** and 8 alien dimensional portals, built for downloadable PC / Steam release.

## Design Documentation
* [01 - Game Vision & Core Architecture](docs/01-game-vision.md) — Core pillars, 3-tier illustrated world, static-frame + `2×2` dynamic token system, combat rules, `1`–`99` progression, shop gear + quest runestone loop, and tech architecture.
* [02 - Art Direction & Verified Token Pipeline](docs/02-art-direction-and-token-pipeline.md) — Stylized high-fantasy digital art guide (`League of Legends` visual benchmark), `#FF00FF` chroma rules, and the verified static-frame + `2×2` portrait token pipeline.
* [03 - Combat System & Stat Tuning](docs/03-combat-and-stats.md) — The 9 damage types, `1`–`99` attribute & XP curves, Silver/Trade Goods economy, combat math, timeline, top-to-bottom party order (`Slots 1–4`), and Resolve rules.
* [04 - World Map of Ethoria, 20 Level Zones & 8 Geographic Regions](docs/04-world-and-realms.md) — `4` connected `4K` maps doubled to `8192×8192` each (`10` Hubs, `5` Zone Outposts & `2` Portals per map = `68` overworld markers `+ #69 Highcrown Keep`), Geographic Regions vs. `20` painted 5-Level Zones (`Z01–Z20`), Ferry Crossings, Sea-Ports, and procedural `1:1` Dungeon & Portal grids.
* [05 - Playable Cast & Classes](docs/05-cast-and-skills.md) — Complete profiles, backstories, Resolve passives, weapon attack modes, **8-Tier (`Pick 2 of 3`) spell pools**, and gear affinities for all 6 heroes (**Valen, Ellowyn, Orin, Rowan, Mera, Harlan**).
* [06 - Gear Suite & Runestones](docs/06-gear-and-runestones.md) — **`24`-Gear-Tier Weapon Suite** (`13` D&D types across `Zones 1–20`), **`23` Shared Armor & Helmet sets**, **`6` Global Shields**, **`6` Class Accessories**, **Pick 1 of 3 Carved Runestones** (`Lesser` $\to$ `Mythic`), and **Town Potions**.
* [07 - Bestiary & The 8 Dimensional Portal Bosses](docs/07-bestiary-and-bosses.md) — Shared-engine enemy rules (`2–3` abilities max), **8 D&D Plane Bosses (ending with King Garrick)**, **120 SRD 5.2 Portal Mobs (`15` per Portal Realm)**, and **8 Ethoria Natural Wildlife beasts**.
* [08 - Quests, Story Arcs & Sandbox Progression](docs/08-quests-and-story.md) — 4-layer sandbox quest system (Open 8-Portal main goal, `4` independent story chains, **`178` handcrafted Hub Runestone quests across `40` Hubs**, and `20` repeatable Zone Outpost Hunts).
* [09 - UI Screens, Controls, Audio Integration & Steam Features](docs/09-ui-and-steam.md) — Top-down & battle HUD, ledger menus, town shops/services & Pick-1-of-3 Runestone draft, audio channel hooks, Save Anywhere JSON schema, and Steamworks / Steam Deck integration.
