# ROD — Project Hub

An original turn-based, illustrated-world RPG set across the human world of **Ethoria** and 8 alien dimensional portals, built for downloadable PC / Steam release.

## Design Documentation
* [01 - Game Vision & Core Architecture](docs/01-game-vision.md) — Core pillars, 3-tier illustrated world, `2×2` dynamic token system, combat rules, `1`–`99` progression, shop gear + quest runestone loop, and tech architecture.
* [02 - Art Direction & Verified Token Pipeline](docs/02-art-direction-and-token-pipeline.md) — Official Hand-Inked Painterly Fantasy style guide, `#FF00FF` chroma rules, and the verified 3-step token prompt workflow (including health-state and Fire spell prompts).
* [03 - Combat System & Stat Tuning](docs/03-combat-and-stats.md) — The 9 damage types, `1`–`99` attribute & XP curves, Silver/Trophy drop economy, combat math, timeline, Front/Back rows, and Resolve rules.
* [04 - World Map of Ethoria, 8 Regions & All 41 Locations](docs/04-world-and-realms.md) — `4` connected `4K` maps doubled to `8192×8192` each (`10` permanent locations & `2` Portals per map), Danger Sub-Zones, 11 towns, ship ports, personal quest landmarks, and procedural Portal Grids.
* [05 - Playable Cast & Classes](docs/05-cast-and-skills.md) — Complete profiles, backstories, Resolve passives, **8-Tier (`Pick 2 of 3`) skill kits**, and weapon/attire affinities for all 6 heroes (**Valen, Ellowyn, Orin, Rowan, Mera, Harlan**).
* [06 - Gear Suite & Runestones](docs/06-gear-and-runestones.md) — Complete **24-step Weapon Suite** (`384` weapons across `16` types — `3+` per class), **24-step Attire & Off-Hand Suite** (`3` shared weights + `5` off-hand types), **Pick 1 of 3 Runestone affix pool**, **16 Universal Sigils**, and **Potions / Camping Supplies** across all 8 Regions.

* [07 - Bestiary & The 8 Dimensional Portal Bosses](docs/07-bestiary-and-bosses.md) — Shared-engine enemy rules (`2–3` abilities max from hero skill pool), **8 D&D Plane Bosses (ending with King Garrick)**, **120 SRD 5.2 Realm Mobs (`15` per Region — world & portal)**, and **16 Ethoria Human/Beast enemies**.
* [08 - Quests, Story Arcs & Sandbox Progression](docs/08-quests-and-story.md) — 4-layer *Skyrim*-style sandbox quest system (Open 8-Portal main goal, 4 independent story chains, **48 standalone Runestone quests** [`67` one-time quests total], and repeatable Town Bounty Boards).
* [09 - UI Screens, Controls, Audio Integration & Steam Features](docs/09-ui-and-steam.md) — Top-down & battle HUD, 6-tab ledger menus, town/Runesmith/Pick-1-of-3 Runestone screens, audio channel hooks, Save Anywhere JSON schema, and Steamworks / Steam Deck integration.
