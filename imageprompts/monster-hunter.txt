# ROD — 16:9 Monster Hunter Palisade Fort Interior Scene Prompts (20 Outposts: Z01–Z20)
# Scope: All 20 Permanent Zone Outposts (5 Per Map · Hunt Quartermaster + Camp Warden Free Rest)
# Design Rule: All 20 Zone Outposts are fortified Palisade Hunting Forts with strictly ENCLOSED INSIDE views (zero outdoor biome landscapes required — matching assets/maps/interiors/ hubs).
# Style Reference: Attach assets/ui/menu_background_16x9_4k_v2.png (color & brushwork match) when generating.
# Art Style Anchor: Exact League of Legends / Legends of Runeterra / Ruined King hand-painted splash style matching assets/maps/interiors/ hubs.
# UI Layout Mandate: Keep the lower foreground (~25–30% height) calm darkly shadowed indoor floor (worn oak planks, dark slate flagstones, sandstone fort tiles, or dark volcanic basalt) so the bottom "People Here" column and Dialogue Plate sit cleanly without blocking key props.
# Hard Rule: Uninhabited eye-level strictly-inside scene — zero people, zero readable writing/letters, zero outer map ring borders.

SHARED STYLE HEADER — CONTINENTS 1–2 & HIGHLAND IRONCREST:
16:9 widescreen high-detail stylized high-fantasy RPG key art splash painting in the exact crisp hand-painted art style of League of Legends (Legends of Runeterra / Summoner's Rift / Ruined King) key art. Dense, razor-sharp opaque digital brushwork brimming with intricate weathered surface textures—chipped and cracked stratified stone blocks, mossy masonry fissures, crooked gnarled tree bark and twisted vine tendons, finely layered leaves and wildflowers, deeply carved architectural relief, crisp sunlit rim highlights, and velvety dark espresso-and-indigo crevice shadows under dramatic warm golden-amber sunset shafts in crystal-clear air.
